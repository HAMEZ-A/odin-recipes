Yam Porridge

Description

Yam porridge, also known as "Asaro" in Nigerian cuisine, is a delicious and comforting dish made from yams cooked in a flavorful tomato-based sauce. It is often seasoned with spices, onions, and sometimes includes vegetables or meat for added richness. The yams are typically peeled, cut into chunks, and simmered until they become soft and tender, absorbing the savory flavors of the sauce. Yam porridge is a popular meal in many West African countries and is enjoyed as a hearty breakfast, lunch, or dinner option.

Ingredients

  • 2 large yams, peeled and cut into chunks
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves of garlic, minced
  • 1 bell pepper, chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 cup of vegetable broth
  • 1 teaspoon of paprika
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Steps

  1. In a large pot, sauté the chopped onion, garlic, and bell pepper until they are softened.
  2. Add the chopped tomatoes and cook until they break down and form a sauce.
  3. Add the yam chunks to the pot and pour in the vegetable broth.
  4. Season with paprika, salt, and pepper. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and let it simmer for about 20-30 minutes, or until the yams are tender and the sauce has thickened.
  5. Serve hot, garnished with fresh herbs if desired.
