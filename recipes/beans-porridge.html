Beans Porridge

Description

Beans porridge is a hearty and nutritious dish made by simmering beans with various ingredients such as vegetables, spices, and sometimes meat. It is a popular comfort food in many cultures around the world, known for its rich flavor and satisfying texture. The dish can be customized with different types of beans, such as black beans, kidney beans, or pinto beans, and can be enjoyed as a main course or a side dish.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup of dried beans (black beans, kidney beans, or pinto beans)
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves of garlic, minced
  • 1 bell pepper, chopped
  • 1 carrot, chopped
  • 2 cups of vegetable broth
  • 1 teaspoon of cumin
  • Salt and pepper to taste

steps

  1. Rinse the dried beans and soak them in water overnight. Drain and rinse before cooking.
  2. In a large pot, sauté the chopped onion, garlic, bell pepper, and carrot until they are softened.
  3. Add the soaked beans to the pot and pour in the vegetable broth.
  4. Season with cumin, salt, and pepper. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and let it simmer for about 1-2 hours, or until the beans are tender.
  5. Serve hot, garnished with fresh herbs if desired.
